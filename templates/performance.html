Why Random Forest?

It achieved the best five-fold cross-validation phishing F1-score (96.58%) and the best holdout phishing recall (96.02%) among Logistic Regression, Random Forest and Gradient Boosting. Gradient Boosting remains a strong comparison model, but it did not win in this experiment.
